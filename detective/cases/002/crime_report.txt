CASE 002
HARRINGTON OFFICE THEFT

At approximately 10:25 PM on September 8, a locked cabinet in the records office was discovered open.

A sealed envelope containing $4,000 in cash was missing.

The cabinet showed signs of forced entry. Damage around the lock suggests a flat metal object was used to pry it open.

The envelope was last confirmed inside the cabinet at 9:50 PM.

Building security automatically locked all exterior employee entrances at 10:30 PM.

No alarm was triggered.
